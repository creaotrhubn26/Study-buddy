# Analysis Reporting Lesson 2.2

Source reviewed while signed in on 9 November 2026: Noroff book 22937, lesson chapters 81722–81726 and Task 2.2 chapter 81727. The linked official solution was not opened. The chocolate-bar workbook belongs to the lesson demonstration and was not substituted for the task data.

The supplied fictional Nice Ride counts are Mercedes 220, Audi 180, Volkswagen 150, Skoda 130, Volvo 100 and Tesla 85. They total 865. The task asks for an MS Word report identifying the highest-sales brand, with heading styles, a Column plot and a short comment after the plot. It specifies no reporting period, shop-level breakdown, word count or page count.

`arp_lesson22.py` supplies original English material, seven steps, qualified worked reasoning and file checks. Its UID keeps the student's draft and selected step separate from other tasks. The normal Lessons and Module 2 Training Center paths both prepare the task in Exam Resolver without automatically requesting an AI answer. Resolver uses the column-chart report profile, the selected step, source counts, limits and learner draft.

The original Word template contains semantic headings, bracketed prose prompts and a static data-chart picture. It was rendered and visually inspected after the final edit. The Excel learning workbook contains numeric inputs and a native clustered column chart bound to the six data rows, plus SUM, MAX and INDEX/MATCH summaries. The artifact engine recalculation was checked by changing Audi's count, confirming the total and leader updated, and restoring the original input. The workbook was rendered and its exported chart direction, source bindings and numeric cells are tested. These checks do not establish every Microsoft 365 edition's behaviour.

Both conceptual illustrations were made using built-in imagegen. The exact generation/edit prompts and credits are in `assets/arp`. The Word chart picture is drawn from the numerical data, not generated by imagegen. Original Noroff documents, screenshots and solutions are not hosted by the app.
