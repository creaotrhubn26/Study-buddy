# Analysis Reporting Lesson 2.4

Reviewed while signed in on 9 November 2026: Noroff book 22937, Lesson 2.4 chapters 81736–81742 and Task 2.4 chapter 81743. Original lesson: https://learning.noroff.no/mod/book/view.php?id=22937&chapterid=81736. Original task: https://learning.noroff.no/mod/book/view.php?id=22937&chapterid=81743.

The task offers a hypothetical team-leader route focused on leadership, real group work, or solo work as Anna, Ben or Chris. Q1 introduces the team and delegates dataset selection to Anna and Chris; Q2 gives Ben the EDA; Q3 assigns Anna report structure/coherence and Chris visuals/explanations; Q4 assigns the leader the stakeholder speech. The speech accompanies the report, is at least two pages long, and has approximate 800–1000-word guidance. Actual pagination must be checked independently. No dataset, results, fixed report word count, chart count, compulsory slide deck or recording upload is supplied.

The shortlist wording is ambiguous: Q1 names Anna and Chris, while the final deliverables refer to each team member proposing 1–2 datasets. The app preserves that uncertainty, starts the blank shortlist with Anna and Chris, allows extra rows and recommends confirming the counting scope. It does not silently impose a fixed total or require a solo role to invent a completed team project.

The English lesson covers audience adaptation, evidence-to-action reasoning, language/voice/tenses, speech preparation/structure, teamwork and traceable handoffs. Seven learning steps map to the four task questions. Eight original Markdown/CSV templates support a communication plan, dataset shortlist, role handoffs, EDA evidence, report outline, speech manuscript, review checklist and downloadable guide. They contain no supplied data or findings. Two built-in imagegen illustrations and their exact prompts/credits are stored under assets/arp.

Lessons, Module 2 Training Center and Exam Resolver share the task material. Drafts and steps use the existing independent per-task persistence. The route/role is recorded at the top of the draft and transferred with the selected step and source limits. Preparing a task makes no automatic AI request. Changing the draft (including role) or step invalidates the previous answer. Original revision questions are labelled Study Buddy material and appended after existing Module 2 questions to preserve their order.

Verification covers blank template contents, image decoding, source/task boundaries, registry order, full Streamlit handoff, regular/strict mocked AI context, role changes, stale answers, persistence and Training Center resources. Live AI response quality, learner calculations, real collaboration, manuscript pagination and speech delivery are not certified by these checks. Learners inspect actual files and submit themselves. The public repository contains original summaries/templates/generated images and source links, not the Noroff book, screenshots, copied original course files or an official solution.
