## Source ambiguities and interpretation

- Review Transition is the task's exact wording; a matching effect name was not verified. The guide requests clarification if absent rather than substituting Reveal.
- Review Date contains years. Average rating based on review data is interpreted transparently as Average Rating grouped by review year.
- Company >3.5, bean origin >3.25 and cocoa >=3.0 are filters on group-average Rating, before display rounding, not filters on individual reviews or on Cocoa Percent.
- The e-commerce prose contains 200,000 versus 2,000 orders and inconsistent department ordering. Calculate from the actual complete source and acknowledge the discrepancy.
- Fruit quantities are supplied exercise values with no population or reporting year. Citrus lemon preserves task wording, not a verified botanical claim.
- Rental district codes, currency and exact listing period are not replaced with guessed metadata.
