### Optional research leads

Original Study Buddy starter cards, not an approved Noroff reading list or an official answer. The examples use one common Harvard author-date pattern; adapt it to the tutor's required variant. No claim is made that the learner has read these papers.

#### S1 · (Kandel et al., 2012)
Kandel, S., Paepcke, A., Hellerstein, J.M. and Heer, J. (2012) 'Enterprise data analysis and visualization: An interview study', IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, 18(12), pp. 2917–2926. doi: 10.1109/TVCG.2012.219.

[Original publication record](https://idl.uw.edu/papers/enterprise-analysis-interviews)

Possible use: Research lead for the enterprise analyst's activities and organisational context. The abstract describes interviews with 35 analysts across 25 organisations; this is a study sample, not a universal definition of the role.

Reading status: Author-hosted abstract and publication/DOI record checked. Read the paper and record the supporting passages before using detailed claims.

#### S2 · (Collier and Powell, 2024)
Collier, C.A. and Powell, A.L. (2024) 'Data analyst competencies: A theory-driven investigation of industry requirements in the field of data analytics', Journal of Information Systems Education, 35(3), pp. 325–376. doi: 10.62273/SPYC4248.

[Original publication record](https://aisel.aisnet.org/jise/vol35/iss3/8/)

Possible use: Research lead for entry-level knowledge, skills and dispositions. Its abstract describes a theory-based model and hiring-manager survey; findings have a study context, not a guarantee of all current employer requirements.

Reading status: Journal-hosted abstract, authors, issue, page range and DOI checked against the journal's publication record. Detailed claims need full-text reading.

#### S3 · (Ghasemaghaei, 2023)
Ghasemaghaei, M. (2023) 'Does knowledge sharing belief of data analysts impact their behavior?', Journal of Computer Information Systems, 63(2), pp. 460–478. doi: 10.1080/08874417.2022.2070799.

[Original publication record](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/08874417.2022.2070799)

Possible use: Research lead for sharing analytical insights in organisations. The publisher's abstract describes a study of knowledge-sharing beliefs and behaviour. Do not assert an intervention causes better decisions from the title alone.

Reading status: Publisher-indexed abstract and bibliographic details checked. The issue is 2023; online-first publication was 2022. Use the year consistently for the version cited. Full text was not reviewed.
