Additional characteristics must be labelled hypothetical; age and hobbies do not establish technical knowledge, preferred chart style or business risk appetite. No market findings, official solution content or researched interview quotations are bundled.

## Lesson 1.2 and Task 1.2

Task 1.2 requires a low-fidelity mobile web sketch with height, weight and age inputs; submission; a chart based on other users' BMI; a highlighted current-user value; chart selection; and filtering such as age range. Six English task steps, a wireframe plan and optional formative usability plan are available in Lessons and Exam Resolver. A two-page editable Word template contains both plans. The student's written plan and selected step transfer without generating an answer. The resolver reviews text plans; drawing pixels are not passed to the model.

Lesson 1.2 explains five ideation techniques, prototype fidelity versus interaction, usability test dimensions and A/B testing. It notes that changing colour and shape simultaneously does not isolate either effect. BMI arithmetic and age-specific interpretation boundaries are checked against the CDC calculation reference. The illustrative image and template use placeholders or explicitly illustrative points, with no real user data, clinical conclusions or invented usability findings. The official solution was not opened.
