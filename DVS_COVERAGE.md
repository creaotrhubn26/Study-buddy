## Source boundaries

The task's presentation concerns market analysis for snow-free bobsleds. It asks for a hypothetical persona of an extreme-sports start-up CEO in his early twenties who enjoys BASE jumping. Additional characteristics must be labelled hypothetical; age and hobbies do not establish technical knowledge, preferred chart style or business risk appetite. No market findings, official solution content or researched interview quotations are bundled.

Activity 1.1.1 instead concerns Funky Monkey, a statement-sock brand seeking recognition among male and female athletes aged 25–35 who participate in mountain biking, triathlons or marathons. The activity and task briefs are kept distinct.
